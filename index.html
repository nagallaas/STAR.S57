📚 False-Positive Handling & Detection Documentation

1. False-positive handling

  • Authorized helpdesk accounts are treated differently from regular employee accounts.
  • Multiple host accesses by helpdesk users during an approved maintenance window receive a risk reduction.
  • Approved maintenance hosts and activities receive additional contextual risk reduction.
  • A new employee location is not automatically treated as highly suspicious when the source IP belongs to a declared company VPN range.
  • Context reduces risk rather than deleting the underlying evidence.

2. Remaining uncertainties

  • A VPN match does not prove that the login was authorized or that the employee personally performed it.
  • Maintenance configuration may become outdated.
  • User baselines may be incomplete when only a small amount of historical activity is available.
  • Timestamp interpretation depends on the timestamp supplied by the log and the browser environment.
  • This application is decision-support software and does not guarantee that an activity is malicious or legitimate.

3. Impact of contextual rules

The detection engine first calculates a raw risk score. It then applies contextual adjustments based on the account role, helpdesk authorization, maintenance window, approved activity, company VPN, known hosts and known locations.

Therefore, a legitimate maintenance operation can have strong raw signals but a lower final risk score, while a regular employee accessing many unfamiliar hosts outside an approved maintenance window remains highly suspicious.

4. Incident correlation

Related alerts from the same user within the configured incident window are grouped together. Alerts can also be related through common IP addresses or hosts. Each incident receives a final risk score, supporting evidence, explanations and investigation guidance.
